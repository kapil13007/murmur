How it works

Three steps, no window to open.

1

Hold

Press and hold Ctrl + Win in any app. A small bar at the bottom of your screen shows that Murmur is listening.

2

Speak

Talk naturally. Say “um”, change your mind, spell out an email address. Murmur sorts it out.

3

Release

Let go and the finished text is pasted at your cursor within a second or two. Your clipboard is left exactly as it was.

What it does

Dictation that reads like you wrote it.

Cleans up as you speak

Removes “um” and “uh”, applies self-corrections (“3pm, actually 4pm”), adds punctuation and builds lists.

Knows where you’re typing

Email addresses come out as name@gmail.com. Subject lines get no full stop. The To box gets only addresses. Chat is casual, email is formal.

Your words, spelled right

Teach it names and jargon in your dictionary, and it learns from your corrections. Say “my calendar link” to paste the whole thing.

Fast

Powered by Whisper large-v3-turbo on Groq. Text is usually in your document a second or two after you let go.

Command Mode

Select text, hold the command shortcut and say “make this more formal” or “turn this into bullet points”.

Private by design

Privacy Mode keeps no recordings. You can auto-delete history after 24 hours, export everything, or delete your account. Your Groq key is encrypted.

Free, with your own key

No subscription. Bring a free Groq key.

Murmur uses Groq for fast speech recognition. Instead of charging you, it uses your own free Groq account, so you pay nothing to Murmur and your usage stays between you and Groq. Setup takes about a minute.

  1. Install Murmur and create your account.
  2. Open console.groq.com/keys, sign in (no card needed) and create a key.
  3. Paste it into Murmur under Settings, then hold Ctrl + Win and talk.
Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. Murmur doesn’t charge anything. You use your own Groq account, which has a generous free tier that comfortably covers everyday dictation.

Windows says “unknown publisher”. Is it safe?

Murmur isn’t code-signed yet, because a signing certificate costs money. Windows shows a SmartScreen warning for any unsigned app. Click More info, then Run anyway. The full source code is on GitHub if you want to check it.

Where does my voice go?

Audio is sent to the Murmur server, which forwards it to Groq using your key and returns the text. Recordings are kept for up to 14 days so you can retry a transcript, unless you turn on Privacy Mode, in which case none are kept. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Does it work on Mac, Linux or my phone?

Not yet. Murmur is for Windows 10 and 11 only.

Which apps does it work in?

Anywhere you can paste text: browsers, Gmail, Slack, Word, VS Code, Notepad and more. A few apps that block pasting, or run as administrator, may not accept it.

Can I change the shortcut?

Yes. Settings lets you set your own keys for dictating, hands-free mode, Command Mode and pasting your last transcript.
